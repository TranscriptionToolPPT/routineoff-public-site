Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

Public checkout and public software downloads are not yet enabled. This policy describes the planned Word Count Tool service and will be reviewed again before paid subscriptions open.

Document processing

Word Count Tool processes selected document content locally on the user's Windows computer. Document contents are not uploaded to the account-control server as part of normal counting.

Account information

The access service may store a username, securely hashed password, account status, expiry date, device identifier, device name, login timestamps, and session-control events. This information is used to provide access, prevent simultaneous use of one account on multiple devices, and allow administrators to revoke access.

Payments

When paid checkout is enabled, payment information will be handled by Lemon Squeezy as the merchant of record. RoutineOff will not store complete payment-card details.

Retention and security

Account information will be retained only while needed to provide the subscription, keep required records, resolve disputes, and protect the service. Reasonable technical safeguards will be used, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

Customers may request correction or deletion of account information where applicable. A verified support contact will be published before public checkout opens.
