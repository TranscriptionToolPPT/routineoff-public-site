Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

This policy explains how RoutineOff handles information for Word Count Tool. The service is currently in a controlled pre-launch period; this policy will be updated with the legal entity details before public paid subscriptions are activated.

Document processing

Word Count Tool processes selected document content locally on the user's Windows computer. Document contents are not uploaded to the account-control server as part of normal counting.

Account information

The access service may store a username, securely hashed password, account status, expiry date, device identifier, device name, application version, login timestamps, and session-control events. This information is used to provide access, prevent simultaneous use of one account on multiple devices, and allow administrators to revoke access.

Support requests

If a user submits a support request, we may receive the request text, optional screenshots, application version, Windows version, system architecture, device identifier, and relevant error details. The support flow does not send document names, document paths, document contents, extracted text, or full OCR results unless the user deliberately includes them in a support message or screenshot.

Payments

When paid checkout is enabled, payment-card information will be collected and processed by the payment provider shown at checkout. RoutineOff will not store complete payment-card numbers or security codes. We may receive subscription, invoice, payment-status, and billing-contact information required to provide the subscription, issue receipts, resolve billing issues, and meet legal obligations.

Retention and security

Account information will be retained only while needed to provide the subscription, keep required records, resolve disputes, and protect the service. Reasonable technical safeguards will be used, but no system can guarantee absolute security.
